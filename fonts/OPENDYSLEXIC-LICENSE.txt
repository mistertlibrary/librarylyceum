From the project's own site: "The newest version of OpenDyslexic now uses the
SIL-OFL license, giving you freedom to use it for personal use, business use,
education, commercial, books, ebook readers, applications, websites, and any
other project or purpose you need."

The faces bundled in lyceum-dyslexic.css (regular and bold woff2, italic and
bold-italic woff) were taken from the copies already self-hosted in the WEHS
guide network rather than downloaded afresh.

That has a consequence worth stating plainly. Only the two woff2 files carry
the newer OFL licensing. The two italic woff files are the older generation:
their own metadata reads "Original Fonts are © Bitstream. OpenDyslexic changes
and additional glyphs by Abelardo Gonzalez are licensed under a Creative
Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported License," with the Bitstream terms at
http://opendyslexic.org/legal/. CC BY 3.0 asks for attribution, which this note
and FONTS.md give. Refetching the two italics from the current project would
put the whole family under one license and would make them woff2 besides.

The full OFL 1.1 text now sits beside this note as OFL.txt, and FONTS.md lists
every family in this folder with the copyright line read out of its own binary.
